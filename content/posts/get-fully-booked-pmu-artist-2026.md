## The Booking Fortress: Locking Down Appointments

Getting leads is only half the battle. The next crucial step is converting that interest into a confirmed, paid appointment. This is where a rock-solid **pmu booking system** becomes your most valuable asset. It's time to eliminate the endless back-and-forth DMs and professionalize your process.

First and foremost, you must require a deposit to secure an appointment. This simple step dramatically reduces no-shows and filters out clients who aren't serious. It signals that your time is valuable and protects your income. A robust system, like the one PMU Society provides, makes taking deposits seamless.

Furthermore, your system should fire off automated reminders. A sequence of reminders—3 days before, 1 day before, and 1 hour before—keeps the appointment top-of-mind for your client and drastically reduces the chance of a forgotten booking. Combine this with a crystal-clear, firmly enforced cancellation policy, and you'll stop bleeding money from last-minute dropouts.
